The preview follow-ups for #3477 (#3486, #3488) and the ffmpeg-free beat render (#3490) are on main but npm still serves 5.4.0.

- The launcher range follows (`^5.5.0`), and the CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]` entry and Ships line are updated.
